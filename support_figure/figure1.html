Suggested caption. Figure 1. Conceptual overview of SUPPORT. Three interconnected work packages examine support seeking and coping across friends and peer networks (WP1), parents (WP2), and digital environments (WP3: social media and AI chatbots). Support in one context may correspond with, supplement, or substitute support in another. These processes are studied in relation to mental health.
