EXPERIMENT OVERVIEW
	In the first setup, the experiment will have a general intro section, followed by a single block of trials.
	The intro section should (for the first setup) contain a character creation screen (as in the Scannimals/ Fennimals experiment), followed by an introduction to a partner. 
	The experiment should then continue with the block of trials.
	
Blocks of trials.
	Each block consits of a predefined sequence of trials. For the first pass, lets do 9 trials in a single block. 
	During each block we offer participants different trials. 
	For this we have an offer-window. For now, lets have this be a simple white background. 
	
	With random interval, the experiment controller should offer a trial. 
	Each trial is defined by two variables: the reward earned for completing the trial (5, 10 or 20 points), and the social context (alone, cooperation-required, cooperation-optional).
	For the first version, lets have one of each trial, in random format.
		
	During each block we offer participants different trials. 
	This offer screen has two points of information, indicated by two icons in a box. This box should be positioned in the center of the screen and be about 600px wide and 300px high. 
	The icons are presented side-by-side on the middle of the box
	On the left is the reward amount, represented by a reward medal. Each reward is represented by a different medal ("point_medals" in the SVG).
	On the right is a social-context icon. These icons do not yet exist, but lets use some placeholder rectangles for now (printed red for alone, blue for cooperation required, green for cooperation-optional).
	
	Below the box are two buttons: accept or reject. 
	If the participant clicks accept, the trial takes places as outlined below. If the player rejects, then pause for a brief duration (uniformly selected from 1 to 2.5 seconds) and offer the next trial in the sequence. 
	
TRIAL OVERVIEW
Trial initialization
	At the start of each trial, the player and partner icon are placed on the screen. These icons need to be copied from the partner SVG (and updated with the correct colors)
	Both have an invisible marker that indicates their feet-position (should represent the middle-bottom of the icon bounding box).
	
	In addition, there should be two ladders: the player ladder and the partner ladder. Both ladders need to be moved from their sublayer. 
	In their SVG group, each ladder has a base point. The invisible elements layer also has a base-point. Both ladders should be placed such that these points are aligned.
	The player ladder should be positioned on top of the tree layer, whilst the partner ladder should be positioned behind the tree layer. 
	
	Then the correct apples should stay visible, whilst all other apples are removed (or at least invisible and inactive for the duration of the trial).
	Which apples are correct depends on the social context of the trial. For alone: the 3 bottom apples should be visible. For cooperation-required the 3 upper apples should be visible, and for cooperation-optional the middle 3 apples should be visible.
	
Task overview	
	During each trial, the player has to clear the apples from a tree. For the two cooperation-based social contexts, this involves help from the partner (the partner remains fixed in position for the alone-type trials).
	At the start of the trial, the player can only move horizontally, using the left and right arrow keys (or a and d). The player is constrained in its movements so it can't walk off-screen in either direction.
	
	First, the player has to move over to near the player-ladder. If the player get sufficiently close to the ladder, then the ladder should light up (using a pulsing dropshadow).
	The player then has to hold space to drag the player-ladder. Once space is released, the player-ladder stops being in its dragging state. As long as space is held, the outline of the player-ladder is highlighted (again, using a dropshadow - but now a fixed one).
	If the player moves whilst the player-ladder is in its dragging state, then the player-ladder moves horitontally with the player (keeping its vertical position fixed)
	The task of the player is to drag the ladder to the player_ladder_tree_base_point. 
	
	Once the player-ladder is sufficiently close to this point (lets say <100px distance), it stops being dragged (or draggable in general) and snaps to this position (so that the base point of the ladder aligns with this point). 
	After snapping to this position, the ladder remains static for the rest of the trial (and is no longer outlined at any point).
	After a brief (500ms) pause to let the ladder-moving animation finish, the player icon moves to the foot of the base (such that the bottom of the icon aligns with the ladder base point). 
	From now on, the player icon can no longer be moved by the participant.
	The player icon is the animated to move up the ladder - moving in a straight line such that the icon feet overlap the top point of the player ladder). 
	
	Once the player icon has finished climbing the ladder, the visible apples should have a highlighted outline.
	The player then has to click each of these apples with the cursor. Clicking an apple will fade it out over 500ms (repeated clicks should not do anything). 
	Once all three apples have been clicked, the trial finishes - see details under "reward"
	
Social context
	The partner's behavior is governed by the social context. For the alone context, the partner icon semi-randomly moves around its starting point - but never ventures too far, and mostly stays unmoved.
	
	For the cooperation-required and cooperation-optional contexts, the partner assists the player by holding down a branch of the tree to lower the apples on this branch. This occurs as following:
	Once the player icon starts moving, the partner is shown to move horitontally to the partner ladder. The partner icon moves to a point which is based on the partner-ladders starting point, with a offset of 200px to the left. 
	Then the partner ladder and the partner icon move horizontally until the partner ladder base point overlaps with the corresponding base point in the invisible elements. 
	The partner ladder then remains fixed for the remainder of the trial.
	
	The partner then climbs the ladder - shown by moving the partner icon in a straight line until the partner's feet line up with the top point of the partner ladder. 
	After a brief (200ms) beat, the partner moves back down the ladder over a 750ms period - moving down to about 25% of the ladder height (the difference between the base point and the top point). 
	Whilst the partner is moving down, the tree branch (in the SVG as "rotation_tree_branch") is rotated from its normal position to a rotation of 45 degrees. This rotation should be centered around a pivot point included in the branch SVG group. 
	
Reward
	At the start of the trial, the correct reward medal should be shown in the top-right part of the screen. 
	After all 3 apples have been picked the screen should fade to black with an animated-in opacity mask (a rect element will do). Only the reward medal should be above this mask. The reward medal should move to the center of the screen. Once there, a quick animation shimmer (as we use in the Fennimals experiment) should highlight the reward. Plus a quick pulse-animation. 
	The points are added to the player's total and the trial is concluded.

Time-out
	Each trial should have a maximum time (lets start by setting this to 10s, declared in the stimulus settings). At the start of the trial, a countdown-circle should appear on the top-left to indicate the remaining time (we can copy this countdown from the Fennimals project).
	Once all apples are collected the timer is frozen, and the clock dissapears.
	If the timer runs to zero before al lthe apples are collected, then the reward medal is flashed in red and fades out (100ms). The trial fades to black and concludes. No points are added to the player's total. 

After the trial is concluded, the screen returns to the empty offer-window. After a brief random delay, offer the next trial.
