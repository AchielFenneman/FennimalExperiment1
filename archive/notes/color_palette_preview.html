Assignment rules (in-game): a box cannot use any hue of regions that share it (union ban); a toy cannot use its own region hue or its box’s hue; near-miss bans also apply — Jungle: teal/cyan/lime; North: teal/cyan; Village: lavender/orange/magenta/pink/coral; Desert: coral/sand (boxes and toys). Boxes also avoid pairwise near-misses with each other (e.g. no sand+brown). Soft muted blue / green / red / yellow may still be used when not banned. This page shows candidate pools per set — not a live assignment.
